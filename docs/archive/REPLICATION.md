Measurements have evaluator averages but are excluded from ratings. Missing rounds are excluded, not zeroed. Program staff perform per-round check-in/scoring; duplicate bib numbers warn without blocking. Program/scorecard changes clear all captured data after warning; deleting a round clears only that round after confirmation, while date/location changes preserve scores. Results expose rounds, combined ratings, evaluator detail, CSV and Team Builder handoff. Exact normalization/round weighting remains unspecified; do not invent parity.

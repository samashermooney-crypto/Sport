# Getting started with Athlentry

When you open the admin console for the first time, the **onboarding checklist** guides you through setup. Each item links to the screen where you complete it, and items check off automatically as you finish them.

## The checklist

1. **Connect payments** — link a Stripe account under Settings → Payments so families can pay online.
2. **Set up users and roles** — invite staff under Settings → Members and assign roles such as registrar or treasurer.
3. **Choose sports** — pick the sports you run under Settings → Sports. This controls the terminology and fields you see everywhere else.
4. **Create a program** — use the program wizard to create a season, league, camp, or class session.
5. **Add facilities** — register the fields, courts, or rinks you play on so scheduling can assign them.
6. **Configure compliance** — require background checks, certifications, or documents for staff roles under Compliance.
7. **Import members** — bring in your existing people, rosters, and history from spreadsheets (see *Importing your data*).
8. **Publish your website** — turn on your public organization site so families can find you.
9. **Open registration** — set registration dates on a program and Athlentry opens registration automatically.

You can dismiss any item that does not apply to you, and dismiss the whole checklist when you are done. Dismissed items stay dismissed.

## Invite your staff early

Registrars, treasurers, and coaches each see a console tailored to their role. Invite them from Settings → Members before opening registration so they can learn the system alongside you.

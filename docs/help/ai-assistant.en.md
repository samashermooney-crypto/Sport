# AI assistance

Athlentry includes optional AI features. They are **off unless your organization enables them** — when no provider is configured, no AI controls appear anywhere and no data leaves your organization.

## What AI can do

- **Draft forms from documents** — upload a PDF, Word, or text file (for example a paper waiver or registration form) and AI drafts an Athlentry form. Nothing is published automatically: you review the draft and apply it yourself.
- **Translate content** — translate announcements, descriptions, and messages between English and Spanish.
- **Family help assistant** — a chat assistant on your public site answers general questions using your published programs, facilities, and help articles. It never answers questions about a specific child, account balance, payment, or medical matter — those are directed to your staff.

## Privacy

- Email addresses and phone numbers are stripped from text before it reaches the AI provider.
- Assistant conversations are kept for 30 days for abuse review, then deleted.
- Every AI call is logged in a usage record with token counts, so you can audit exactly what was used.
- Per-organization usage caps prevent runaway costs.

## Turning it on

AI is enabled per environment by your platform administrator (the `AI_PROVIDER` setting). When enabled, the controls appear in Console → Forms, message composers, and your public site's help widget.

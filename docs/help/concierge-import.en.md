# Concierge import service

If you would rather not run imports yourself — or your old system produces messy exports — the Athlentry team will do the migration with you.

## How it works

1. Open **Help → Contact support** (or the concierge option on the Imports page) and choose **Concierge import**.
2. Tell us which system you are coming from and roughly how many people and seasons you have.
3. We reply with a secure upload link for your export files. Never email files containing children's data.
4. We run the imports in your organization on a screen-shared call, review duplicates with you, and confirm the results together.

## What to prepare

- All CSV/Excel exports you can get from your current system.
- A list of your programs and seasons.
- 45–60 minutes for the call.

There is no charge for concierge import during onboarding.

# Using the family portal

The family portal shows information linked to your verified account and household. Use the organization switcher when you belong to more than one organization, and contact the organization if a child or registration is missing.

Only share information that the organization requests. Do not send medical records, passwords, or payment details through a support request. For a billing question, use the finance page for the relevant registration or ask organization staff.

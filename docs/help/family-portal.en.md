# Using the family portal

The family portal is where you register athletes, sign waivers, pay, and keep your household information current.

## Your household

Everyone in your family lives under one household — guardians, players, and emergency contacts. Add a new player under **Household → Add player** before registering.

## Registering

1. Open **Registration** and pick a program.
2. Choose which player is registering, answer the program's questions, and sign any required waivers.
3. Pay at checkout or choose a payment plan if the program offers one.

## Payments and balances

**Billing** shows open invoices, scheduled installments, and payment history. Update a saved card under **Billing → Payment methods**. Receipts are emailed after every payment.

## Need help?

Use the help assistant on your organization's website for general questions. For anything about your child's specific registration, balance, or a refund, contact the organization directly — the assistant cannot see your account.

# Switch to Athlentry from SportsEngine

This guide does not assume which export screens, labels, columns, or record types are available in a particular SportsEngine account. Options can depend on the account and may change. Use the export tools available to your organization, and contact SportsEngine support if you cannot find a needed export. Never share your SportsEngine password with Athlentry staff.

## Plan the move

- Choose a cutover date and decide which current and historical seasons you need.
- Set up sports, programs, facilities, and compliance requirements in Athlentry before importing records that refer to them.
- Keep an unchanged, access-controlled copy of each source export. Work from copies and limit exports to the data you need.

## Gather available records

Where your account provides them, export records for people and households, registrations, teams and rosters, schedules, facilities, historical payment activity, credentials, and volunteer hours. Availability and file format vary. Do not assume a data type or a specific column is present.

Review signed waivers and other compliance records separately. Import a signature or credential only when the original record, signer, scope, date, and verification status can be preserved and checked. If that cannot be established, ask your compliance lead whether a new acknowledgment is required; do not mark it complete by assumption.

## Import and verify

Use the generic Athlentry templates as a guide. Map the actual headers in each export; there is no SportsEngine-specific preset unless one has been documented from a verified sample for your account. Import facilities and people before schedules and rosters, review every preview and warning, and reconcile totals after each commit.

Historical payment rows are imported as external history. They do not create a charge or move money. Compare imported totals with your source records before using them for reconciliation.

## Cut over

Ask staff to review a sample of family, team, schedule, and payment history records. Update links and communications after the review, then open new registration when your organization is ready. Retain the source exports under your normal privacy and retention controls.

## Get help

Use Contact support in Athlentry to request migration help. For concierge assistance, tell us which record types you need help mapping. Do not email files containing children's personal information; wait for an approved secure transfer method.
